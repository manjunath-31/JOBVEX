Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains privacy practices for the JOBVEX website. Review it against the services, hosting, and data practices used on the live site before publishing.

Information and site operation

JOBVEX displays job listings, articles, and community features. The site may process information needed to deliver pages and operate its services, such as requests sent to its hosting and application servers. The contact form in the current site interface does not transmit or store submissions.

Advertising and cookies

When enabled, JOBVEX uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, and personalize ads, subject to your region and consent choices. Learn more in Google's advertising privacy information and manage ad personalization in Google My Ad Center. You can also manage or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Where required, advertising and measurement technologies should only operate after the applicable consent choices have been collected. Ad personalization and consent settings are also managed through the site's configured consent management platform.

Third-party services

Links to employer career pages and other external websites are governed by those sites' own privacy policies. Google services are governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Your choices and contact

You can control cookies through your browser and manage Google ad personalization through Google My Ad Center. For privacy questions, contact support@jobvex.com. Update this contact address if it is not monitored by the site operator.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The date above indicates the latest revision.
